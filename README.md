# tn-valley-cold-front-era5
ERA5/MetPy case study of the 18–19 October 2025 Tennessee Valley cold front. A 0.25° regional subset shows no front at 06 UTC on the 18th, then a shallow, advective passage on the 19th: about 8–9 K of 850 hPa cooling in 6 hours, collocated with cold advection, frontogenesis, a mixing-ratio drop, and a wind shift, confined below 700 hPa.
